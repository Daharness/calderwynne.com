Our method

A clear process, no surprises.

You know at every stage what we have found and what remains to be done.

  1. Kick-off. We agree the scope, the timetable and the information request list.
  2. Information received. Data room access and first management sessions.
  3. Analysis. Earnings, cash, net debt and working capital, from the ledger up.
  4. Regular calls. Red flags shared as soon as we see them, not at the end.
  5. Draft report. Key findings first, each with its amount and its source.
  6. Final report. Comments addressed, data book delivered.
  7. After the report. Support on Q&A, the negotiation and the SPA.
A finding that moves the price comes first, in plain words, with its amount.
